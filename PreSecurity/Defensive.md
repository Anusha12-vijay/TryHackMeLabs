## What is defensive security?

Defensive security teams play a pivotal role in protecting the network and organisation across globes.
It is also known as Blue team. Major 2 roles are 

1. Preventing intrusions from occuring
2. Detecting when they occur and responding properly.

Major tasks involved in defensive security:

1. Cybersecurity training awareness such as educating users about phishing and social engineering.
2. Documenting and Managing Assets.
3. Preventating security: Firewalls and IPS are literally first line of defense. These control what traffic is allowed to enter and what not.
4. Frameworks,Policies and Procedures: Creating robust policies
5. Comprehensive logging of the network and system activity is essential in detecting the threat or any kind of unauthorised activity.

Various areas of defensive security which make up the defensive team is:
Security Operations Center

SOC is a team of cybersecurity professionals which monitor the network or system to find threats
